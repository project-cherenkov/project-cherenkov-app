Project Cherenkov is preparing a new batch of astronomy questions to expand the practice available in the quiz bank.

The questions cover a range of topics in the astronomy syllabus. Before they are added, they are being checked for scientific accuracy, answer correctness, difficulty and alignment with the syllabus.

We are targeting Sunday, 29 November 2026, for the first addition. The final number of questions and topic coverage may change as the review is completed; only questions that pass the checks will be added.

This is an addition to the existing question bank, not a change to quiz scoring or the learning-plan system. Existing quizzes remain available while the new questions are being prepared.

We will update this notice when the new questions are available.
