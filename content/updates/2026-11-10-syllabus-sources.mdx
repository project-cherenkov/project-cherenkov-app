title: "Each syllabus topic now says where it comes from"
summary: "Physics, informatics and astronomy topics are labelled with the syllabuses that list them, and every subject page opens with its sources."
date: "2026-11-10"
type: syllabus
language: en
---

Each syllabus page now opens with a **Where this comes from** block. It names every source, who issues it, the edition, its official language, when the topic list was last compared with it, and how far that comparison went.

Every topic carries a label showing which syllabuses list it. For physics we added the IPhO syllabus next to the OSN guide: topics only the IPhO lists are labelled as such, and are **not confirmed in the OSN guide**. For informatics we added the IOI syllabus, and for astronomy the 2025 OSN guide, which says astronomy follows the IOAA syllabus.

Nothing about the study planner or the quiz bank changed. They still use only the OSN topics.
