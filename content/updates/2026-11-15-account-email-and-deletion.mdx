Three changes to accounts, all on your **Account** page.

**Verify your email.** New accounts receive an email with a link to confirm the address. The Account page shows whether your email is verified and lets you send the email again. You can still sign in and use the planner and quizzes without verifying. Verification is required for editor access: an account only gets editing tools once its email is verified.

**Password changes.** When your password is changed or reset, we email you a notice saying when it happened. Changing or resetting your password also signs you out of your other devices.

**Delete your account.** You can delete your account yourself. We email a confirmation link first, and nothing is deleted until you open it. Deleting removes your account and the study data stored with it, such as quiz answers, study sessions and planner settings. This cannot be undone.
