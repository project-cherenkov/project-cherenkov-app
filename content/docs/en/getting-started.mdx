Project Cherenkov is an Indonesian olympiad (OSN) editorial archive and study planner for **informatics, physics and astronomy**. Each editorial is a rigorous write-up of one idea — a proof, a derivation, a technique — and every one ships with a working interactive visualization you can manipulate, not a decorative picture.

You do not need an account to read anything. Accounts only unlock the optional [study planner](/docs/study-planner).

## The three sections

The site is organised around the official OSN syllabus. Three sections hang off it:

| Section | What it is | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Syllabus** | The official scope for each subject, topic by topic, showing which topics already have a material and editorials. | Seeing what you are expected to know. |
| **Materials** | Short, unsigned breakdowns of individual syllabus topics. No visualization requirement. | Revising a topic quickly. |
| **Editorials** | The archive proper: a full proof plus an interactive visualization. | Understanding *why* something is true, or why a common mistake is a mistake. |

Open any of them from the **Archive** entry in the navigation bar.

## How editorials are organised

The editorial archive is indexed by **principle** (the general idea an editorial teaches) and **error type** (the specific mistake it corrects) — deliberately not by chapter. That way you can arrive because you made a particular mistake, or because you want to understand a particular idea. Use the filters at the top of the archive to narrow by subject, principle or error type.

If you would rather browse by topic, start from the syllabus instead.

## Languages

The interface is available in Indonesian and English; switch with the **ID / EN** control in the header. Editorials and materials are written in a single language and are shown as written in both interfaces.
