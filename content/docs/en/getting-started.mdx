Project Cherenkov is a learning archive for olympiad informatics, physics and astronomy, with an Indonesia-first OSN pathway. Its syllabus maps the topics, materials establish the concepts behind them, and editorials investigate particular ideas through interactive visualizations and rigorous reasoning.

You do not need an account to read anything. Accounts only unlock the optional [study planner](/docs/study-planner).

## The three sections

The site follows the Indonesian OSN syllabus, alongside the international ones where they help (IPhO, IOI, IOAA), and three sections hang off it:

| Section | What it is | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Syllabus | The scope map for each subject, with topic provenance and links to available materials and editorials. | Seeing what is in scope and finding related learning content. |
| Materials | Concise, unsigned explanations of a syllabus topic or subtopic: definitions, key results, derivations, procedures and common traps. Visualizations are optional. | Establishing or revising the underlying concept. |
| Editorials | Focused investigations of a question, case, phenomenon or problem, developed through an interactive visualization and a complete argument. | Seeing how an idea works in a particular situation. |

Open any of them from the Archive entry in the navigation bar.

## How materials and editorials fit together

Materials and editorials are complementary, not a required sequence. A material explains the overall concept of a syllabus topic or subtopic. An editorial explores a particular question or situation in which an idea becomes visible. An editorial should add an investigation or insight, rather than simply repeat a material. A topic may have a material, an editorial, both or neither.

The editorial archive is indexed by **principle**, the general idea an editorial teaches. It may also be indexed by **error type** when the editorial addresses a specific misconception; not every editorial needs one. Use the filters at the top of the archive to narrow by subject, principle or error type.

To browse by syllabus topic, start from the syllabus.

## Languages

The interface is available in Indonesian and English. Switch with the ID / EN control in the header. Each editorial and material is written in one language and appears as written in both interfaces.
