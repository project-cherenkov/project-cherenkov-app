# Design, accessibility, SEO / AI-search and legal audit

Date: 31 October 2026. Method: the app was run locally (Next dev server), measured in headless Chromium at 1280, 1024, 768 and 390 px, checked with axe-core (WCAG 2.0 to 2.2 AA plus best practices, light and dark, seven pages), and its rendered `<head>`, JSON-LD, robots.txt and sitemap were inspected with indexing switched on. A production build could not be run in the sandbox (Google Fonts are fetched at build time), so nothing here was verified against a production build. Unit tests (101 files, 884 tests), `tsc` and `next lint` pass. This is not legal advice.

## 1. The shifting hero

Observation: at 1280 px the H1 moved from y=218 to 240 to 129 as the carousel changed slide, and the hero height went 709, 753, 532 px. On a phone the content below the hero moved by about 300 px.

Cause: the hero grid vertically centres the text against the right-hand column, and that column was as tall as the single visible simulation (three quite different heights). Two smaller contributors: the trajectory legend row was removed when the angle crossed 45 degrees, and the binary-search note changed height as it wrapped. In addition `HomeVisualizations` was rendered twice (one copy hidden by CSS per breakpoint), so every simulation was mounted twice and its headings duplicated.

Change: all slides share one grid cell and inactive ones are `invisible`, `inert` and `aria-hidden`, so the stage is always as tall as the tallest slide; the component is rendered once and placed by the grid; frames stretch to equal height with playback controls pinned to the bottom; the legend row and step notes reserve their space; a `min-height` matches the loaded height so the stage does not jump about 500 px when the engine chunks replace their skeletons. Result: constant stage height across slides (599 px, 657 px on a phone). Also: slide dots enlarged to 40 px hit areas (WCAG 2.5.8), carousel roles and a status announcement added, binary-search notes moved into the message catalogue (they were English-only on `/id`).

Trade-off: the stage is now as tall as the tallest slide, so the short binary-search card has spare space (its content is centred in it). If that is too tall for the hero, shorten the orbital slide (drop the light curve) rather than reverting the stacking.

## 2. Accessibility

axe-core found no colour-contrast violations. Fifteen contrast checks were inconclusive (mostly text over the hero gradient); they were not checked by hand. Fixed: language switcher failed label-in-name (accessible name now starts with the visible code), the Neutrino dock sat outside any landmark, slide dots were 10 px targets. Re-running axe on home (en, id), privacy and signup in both themes and at two widths reports no violations. Not changed: the decorative "readme.md" / "requirements.txt" eyebrows are read aloud by screen readers; the editorial config-error box uses fixed red without a dark variant; the trajectory readout ("range", "max height") is English on Indonesian pages.

## 3. SEO and AI search

Already good: canonicals, hreflang with x-default, Open Graph, JSON-LD, sitemap, `llms.txt`, and fixtures kept out of the index. Changed: the Indonesian home title and description now contain "OSN"; the About page has a dedicated meta description (it used the neutrino paragraph); `robots.ts` names AI crawlers explicitly (search and answer bots allowed; training bots allowed by default, switchable with `AI_TRAINING_CRAWLERS=disallow`); a build-time warning when indexing is on but `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` is unset; privacy and terms pages added to the sitemap. Not changed, recommended: Archive and Syllabus descriptions are short (52 and 65 characters); the whole message catalogue (about 34 KB) is serialised into every page, including placeholder strings, so passing only the namespaces each page needs would cut HTML weight.

## 4. Legal (items for the owner and a lawyer)

1. Neutrino sends users' chat messages to Google's Gemini API. Google's Gemini API Additional Terms require users to be 18 or over and restrict use in services directed at, or likely to be accessed by, under-18s (read from search results; versions differ, check the current text). Olympiad students are mostly in secondary school. This is the highest-risk item. Options: limit Neutrino to verified adults, switch to a provider whose terms allow minors, or obtain written clarification from Google. Not changed in code; marked with an OWNER DECISION comment in the policy.
2. Indonesia's PP 17/2025 (PP Tunas) is reported (news coverage, not read in the regulation) to be in force from March 2026 and to set duties, including age handling and parental consent, for electronic systems children may use.
3. The old privacy policy omitted Gemini, Google sign-in, hosting and database providers, controller identity, legal basis, retention, rights, cross-border transfer and children, and treated continued use as acceptance. Rewritten in English (`PRIVACY.md`, `TERMS.md`) and Indonesian (`content/legal/id/`), and published on the site at `/privacy` and `/terms`. UU PDP (Law 27/2022) is referenced from general knowledge and was not re-verified here.
4. Signup now shows a notice linking the terms and privacy policy. It is notice, not an age gate or verified consent.
5. No content licence exists for editorials (MIT covers code only). The terms say all rights remain with authors until you choose a licence (marked OWNER DECISION).
6. The owner's personal email appears in `PRIVACY.md` and the team data. Left as is; consider a role address only.
