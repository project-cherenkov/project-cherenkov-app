# Privacy Policy

This is a starter privacy policy for Project Cherenkov, a student project. It is not legal advice.

## 1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features in use, we may collect the following:

- basic account information for sign-in and planner features
- email addresses for authentication and contact
- usage data needed to operate the website and study tools
- optional content submitted through forms or uploads

If you have an account and use the study tools, we also store:

- your quiz answers, including which choice you picked on each question and any retries of missed questions
- your study sessions (when you studied, for how long, and the subject or topic you chose), whether from the timer or entered by hand
- the pages you marked as "understood"
- your planner settings and topic self-ratings

You can see all of this on your Progress and Missed questions pages, and you can delete individual study sessions there. Visitors without an account can use the study timer, but nothing is sent to us or saved. The "try it first" preference, the timer's state while it runs, and site search all stay in your own browser; what you type into search is not sent to a server.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to do the following:

- operate the site and required account features
- provide study planner and quiz functionality
- respond to user questions or support requests
- improve the project and content quality

## 3. Cookies and analytics

If analytics or cookies are enabled in the future, they may be used to understand traffic patterns. Any cookie system will be disclosed in the site footer or settings page.

## 4. Data Storage

We store data only as needed to provide the project's features. Access is limited to the project team and authorized administrators.

## 5. Third-Party Services

This project may use hosting, authentication, analytics or storage providers. Those services may process information under their own policies.
