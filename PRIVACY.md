# Privacy Policy

This document is a simple starter privacy policy for Project Cherenkov. It is not legal advice, but it is a clear and reasonable default for a student project.

## 1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features in use, we may collect:
- basic account information for sign-in and planner features
- email addresses for authentication and contact
- usage data needed to operate the website and study tools
- optional content submitted through forms or uploads

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:
- operate the site and required account features
- provide study planner and quiz functionality
- respond to user questions or support requests
- improve the project and content quality

## 3. Cookies & Analytics

If analytics or cookies are enabled in the future, they may be used to understand traffic patterns and improve the experience. If a cookie system is used, it should be clearly disclosed in the site footer or settings page.

## 4. Data Storage

We will store data only as needed to provide the project's features. Access is limited to the project team and authorized administrators where applicable.

## 5. Third-Party Services

This project may use services such as hosting, authentication, analytics, or storage providers. Those services may process information under their own policies.

This privacy policy may be updated as the project grows. Continued use of the site after updates means you accept the new policy.
